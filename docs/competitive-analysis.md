# ONE — Competitive Analysis

## 1. Overview of the Competitive Landscape
The digital productivity and screen time market has matured into three primary segments:
1. **Operating System Defaults** (Apple Screen Time, Google Digital Wellbeing)
2. **Hard Blockers & Gamified Clocks** (Freedom, Opal, Forest, Cold Turkey)
3. **Friction-Based Interventions** (one sec, ScreenZen, Present)

---

## 2. In-Depth Competitor Breakdown

### 2.1 Apple Screen Time & Android Digital Wellbeing
* **Strengths:** System-level integration, zero battery penalty, pre-installed on billions of devices.
* **Weaknesses:**
  * Passive surveillance: Measures presence ("6h 12m screen time") without understanding context or purpose.
  * Frictionless dismissal: "Ignore limit for today" is just two quick taps, eroding self-regulation over time.
  * Absence of intention: Viewing an educational tutorial on YouTube is tracked identically to doomscrolling reels.

### 2.2 Forest
* **Strengths:** Friendly visual metaphor, social virality, tree-planting charitable contribution.
* **Weaknesses:**
  * Gamified punishment: Killing a tree triggers guilt and anxiety.
  * Rigid all-or-nothing model: If an emergency call arrives, the tree dies, creating frustration.
  * No distinction between automatic urges and genuine intentional research needs.

### 2.3 Opal & Freedom
* **Strengths:** Strong network-level/VPN blocking on multiple devices, scheduled recurring sessions.
* **Weaknesses:**
  * Adversarial relationship: "Access Denied" screens provoke reactance and bypass attempts.
  * High subscription cost ($80–$100/yr), paywalling basic functionality.
  * No cognitive offloading: Users with stray thoughts either break focus to write them down elsewhere or get distracted.

### 2.4 "one sec" & ScreenZen
* **Strengths:** Excellent behavioral grounding; uses 10-second delays (breathing exercises) to break automatic dopamine loops. Backed by PNAS 2023 study.
* **Weaknesses:**
  * App-centric rather than intention-centric: It delays app opens across the day, but lacks an active "ONE THING" focus state.
  * No concept of a session goal: It cannot ask "You said you'd finish your DSA assignment — why are we opening Safari?"
  * Lacks outcome verification and structured distraction recovery.

## 3. The ONE Strategic Moat

| Pillar | Industry Standard | ONE Advantage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Foundation** | Restricting apps | Protecting specific user intention |
| **Intervention** | Hard error / Breathing screen | Intent Firewall with contextual dialogue |
| **Exceptions** | Unlimited bypass or total lockout | Controlled, time-boxed Intentional Exception passes |
| **Distraction Drift** | Marked as "Failure" | Measured as "Recovery Speed" (Non-judgmental) |
| **Cognitive Buffer** | None (forces distraction) | Distraction Inbox (Zeigarnik offloading) |
| **Output Metric** | Screen hours reduced | Tangible outcomes completed |
| **Privacy** | Cloud telemetry & sync | 100% on-device local-first storage |
