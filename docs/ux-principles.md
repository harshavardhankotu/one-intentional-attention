# ONE — UX Principles & Design System

> **Calm, intentional, non-judgmental.** The interface must feel like a sanctuary for the mind, never a demanding taskmaster or a flashy video game.

---

## 1. Visual Identity
* **Atmosphere:** Deep focus, meditative clarity, tactile quietness.
* **Palette:**
  * **Surfaces:** Obsidian `#090A0F`, Deep Slate `#12151F`, Card Surface `#1A1E2E`, Subtle Border `#2A3047`.
  * **Typography:** Pure White `#FFFFFF` (headings), Muted Silver `#94A3B8` (body), Subtle Zinc `#64748B` (metadata).
  * **Intent Accent:** Warm Amber `#F59E0B` (intention spotlight, active pass).
  * **Calm Accent:** Forest Sage `#10B981` (peaceful recovery, completion).
  * **Firewall Intercept:** Soft Persimmon `#F97316` (mindful pause without alarmist red error colors).

---

## 2. Interaction Design Rules
1. **Zero Clutter in ONE THING MODE:**
   * When focusing, all menus, sidebars, charts, and notifications disappear.
   * Only the user's chosen goal, remaining time, progress arc, and minimal controls remain.
2. **Friction with Compassion:**
   * When the user attempts to drift, the dialog does not accuse them.
   * Copy: *"WAIT. You said: [Goal]. 42 minutes remain. What happened?"*
   * Clean typography, large touch targets, keyboard-friendly options (`1`, `2`, `3`).
3. **Instant Cognitive Relief:**
   * Floating or hotkey-driven Distraction Inbox (`Cmd/Ctrl+K` or click `Save for Later`).
   * One-line input. Pressing Enter immediately dismisses the prompt and returns to focus with a calming haptic chime.
4. **Outcome Celebration Without Addiction:**
   * When a session finishes, celebrate the tangible outcome achieved.
   * No spinning wheels, flashing confetti, or streak countdown pressure.
